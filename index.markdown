I'm a postdoctoral research associate in Simon Levin's lab at the High Meadows Environmental Institute, Princeton University, working on the feedback between predator group formation and prey populations.

## Education

Dissertation: *The evolution of social learning by predators in predator-prey systems.* Advisor: Prof. Marcus Feldman.

**B.A., Mathematics & Biology** — Swarthmore College, 2014–2018.
Visiting Semester at Ben Gurion University, January–July 2017.
