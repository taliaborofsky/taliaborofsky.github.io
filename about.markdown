I'm a postdoctoral research associate at the High Meadows Environmental Institute, Princeton University, working on the evolution of social learning, cooperative hunting, and adaptive behavior in predator-prey systems.

## Education

**Ph.D., Biology** — Stanford University, 2018–2023
Dissertation: *The evolution of social learning by predators in predator-prey systems.* Advisor: Prof. Marcus Feldman.

**B.A., Mathematics & Biology** — Swarthmore College, 2014–2018
Visiting Semester at Ben Gurion University, January–July 2017.
